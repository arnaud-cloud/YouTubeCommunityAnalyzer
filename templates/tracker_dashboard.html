Growth Leaders

Total Views Growth (%) ℹ% increase in total views from the first to the latest snapshot. Measures raw audience reach expansion — how fast a channel is accumulating viewers over time.

Avg Views / Video Growth (%) ℹ% change in average views per video. Separates channels that grow by publishing more content from those whose individual videos genuinely perform better over time.

Subscriber Growth (%) ℹ% increase in subscriber count from first to latest snapshot. Subscribers are a channel's loyal core audience — growth here signals long-term health and sustained interest.

Engagement Rate (%) ℹLikes ÷ Views × 100, from the latest snapshot. Filters passive views from genuine approval — a high rate means the audience is invested, not just scrolling past. Size-agnostic: comparable across channels of any scale.

Views per Subscriber ℹTotal views ÷ total subscribers, from the latest snapshot. Measures how effectively a channel converts its subscriber base into actual viewers. A high ratio means subscribers keep coming back; a low ratio signals a large but passive audience.

Comment Rate (%) ℹComments ÷ Views × 100, from the latest snapshot. Comments require more effort than likes, so this measures genuine community interaction. Channels with a high comment rate are provoking real conversation, not just passive consumption.
